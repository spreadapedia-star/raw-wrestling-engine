HISTORICAL TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE-CHANGE REFERENCE
Research window: 1980-1995; ECW through 1999. Mid-South/UWF and WCW continuity are identified rather than silently mixed.
Compiled 2026-11-17 for a wrestling video game. Plain text / UTF-8.

EVIDENCE AND UNCERTAINTY RULES
Dates, champion names and cities below come from the cited wrestling-titles.com historical title-history pages.
OPPONENTS are specified as VERIFIED FROM LINEAGE only if the source specifically says whom the winners defeated. Otherwise: UNVERIFIED. Prior listed holders are provided as a lead only, NOT evidence of a match.
VENUE means the actual building/arena, not the municipality. Most lineage pages give city but not arena: VENUE = UNVERIFIED. Do not assume a city's usual arena.
A partial month/year date is exactly the level of precision in the source; do not invent a day. A * or ? on source date/place indicates uncertainty and is flagged.
Sources are secondary title-lineage compilations, not independent primary event-result checks. Even a sourced date is a lineage assertion, not automatically a verified match finish.
Title vacancy/award/held-up and succession notes are marked when documented in the source, but a vacancy is not silently treated as a match victory.
Each entry carries its individual source URL. A sourced champion/date/city does NOT mean the missing opponent, arena or finish has been established.
Source ordering and event taping/broadcast chronology may differ; entries are sorted by the SOURCE-REPORTED date within each subsection.

KNOWN CONFLICTS / SPECIAL CASES
MEMPHIS SHEEPHERDERS: The wrestling-titles.com Southern page records Jonathan Boyd and Luke Williams as 1983-12-13, out of sequence. Other histories give 1982-12-13 (https://www.prowrestlinghistory.com/memphis/titles/southerntag.html) or 1982-12-27 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AWA_Southern_Tag_Team_Championship). DATE UNVERIFIED / CONFLICTING. Do not treat 1983-12-13 as established.
WCW EARLY 1991: The Steiner Brothers' 1991-02-18 television taping occurred before the 1991-02-24 Freebirds title victory was aired/recognized; wrestling-titles.com explicitly discusses this unusual ordering. Preserve both records and flag the chronology rather than invent another date.
NWA/WCW 1992: Gordy and Williams won the WCW tag title on 1992-07-05 and revived NWA recognition on 1992-07-12, separate title-change actions.
